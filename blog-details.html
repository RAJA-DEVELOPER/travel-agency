The Ultimate Guide to Greek Island Hopping in Luxury

Elena Vasquez
Lead Travel Writer
July 15, 2024 12 min read

Greece's island archipelago remains one of the world's most romantic and visually spectacular destinations. Yet navigating its 6,000 islands with the luxury and ease you deserve requires expert knowledge. Here is our definitive guide.

Why Island Hop in Style?

The traditional island-hopping route favored by backpackers — crowded ferries, busy hostels, rushed itineraries — couldn't be further from the Luxe Voyages experience. We believe the Greek islands deserve to be savored, not rushed through. Our curated approach places you aboard private catamarans, in cliff-top suites, and at sunset dining tables reserved weeks in advance.

The key difference in luxury island hopping lies in access. Our partnerships with boutique properties and local experts open doors that simply aren't available to the general traveler — private vineyard tours in Santorini, after-hours visits to the Palace of Knossos, and exclusive beach clubs on Mykonos's most secluded coves.

Santorini's iconic caldera at golden hour — the perfect luxury island backdrop.

The Ideal Luxury Route

Days 1–3: Santorini

Begin your journey in Santorini, the jewel of the Cyclades. We recommend staying in Oia's cave hotels — preferably at one of the clifftop suites with private plunge pool and caldera views. Wake to sunrise over the volcanic crater, enjoy a private catamaran tour of the caldera's hot springs, and end each evening with dinner at one of the island's celebrated sunset-view restaurants.

The moment your catamaran rounds the caldera's edge and Santorini's white-washed cliffs come into full view is one of travel's great revelations.

Days 4–6: Mykonos

From Santorini, transfer by private speedboat to Mykonos — a journey of approximately three hours that is itself an experience. The glittering Aegean, the distant silhouettes of islands, and the freshness of sea air set the tone for this cosmopolitan island famous for its vibrant atmosphere and world-class beaches.

In Mykonos, we place our clients in the island's finest boutique properties — intimate, architecturally stunning hotels where discretion and personalized service define every moment. Days are spent at private beach clubs; evenings unfold at waterfront tavernas serving the freshest octopus and local wines.

Days 7–8: Paros

Escape to Paros for a more serene conclusion — marble villages, turquoise bays, and an authentic Greek island pace untouched by mass tourism. This is where you truly disconnect.

"Greece doesn't need improvement. It needs to be experienced slowly, deeply, and with someone who knows where to take you."

Practical Luxury Tips

  • Best months: May–June and September–October for ideal weather and fewer crowds
  • Getting between islands: Private speedboats or small chartered planes eliminate ferry queues entirely
  • Book ahead: The finest Santorini suites often book 12+ months in advance
  • Local currency: Carry Euros; many private experiences prefer cash for tips
  • Dress code: Smart casual at most luxury venues; some require formal dress for dinner

Our Recommended Luxury Properties

After 19 years of sending clients to Greece, we maintain relationships with only the finest properties. Our curated list includes boutique cave hotels in Oia, clifftop villa collections in Mykonos, and secluded beach resorts in Paros — all pre-inspected by our team and meeting our exacting luxury standards.
