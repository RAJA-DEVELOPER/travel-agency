Luxe Voyages Ltd. ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on the site includes personal data you voluntarily provide when registering, booking a tour, or contacting us:

  • Identity Data: Full name, username, date of birth, and nationality
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone number, and billing address
  • Passport & Travel Data: Passport number, expiry date, visa information
  • Financial Data: Payment card details (processed via secure third-party payment processors — we do not store full card numbers)
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, products and services

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect or receive to provide, improve, and personalize our services. Specific uses include:

  • Processing and fulfilling your travel bookings and reservations
  • Communicating with you regarding your bookings, enquiries, and account
  • Sending marketing communications where you have opted in
  • Improving our website functionality and user experience
  • Complying with our legal and regulatory obligations

3. Data Sharing

We may share your personal information with third parties in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Hotels, airlines, tour operators, and other travel suppliers necessary to fulfill your booking
  • Payment Processors: Secure third-party payment gateways that process your transactions
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our legal rights

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our website and hold certain information. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our service.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your personal data

6. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. These include SSL encryption, regular security audits, and strict access controls.
